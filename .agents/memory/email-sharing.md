---
name: Email sharing and clipboard
description: Why profile email preparation and formatted copy are separate actions.
---

Keep “Préparer un courriel” and “Copier la fiche mise en forme” as distinct actions. The mailto draft can prefill the subject and body. Rich clipboard copying can provide HTML and plain text for the message body, but cannot set the subject or control where the receiving mail client inserts content relative to its signature.

**Why:** Outlook controls the insertion point and signature behavior; pasting at the end of a draft can place a profile below the signature.

**How to apply:** Keep labels and instructions explicit. Use the mailto action when the user wants a prefilled draft; explain that clipboard copy is body-only and must be pasted at the desired cursor position.
